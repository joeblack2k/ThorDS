# Known stress failures to reproduce

## AYN Thor reports

- Chain Chomp proximity;
- Bob-omb Battlefield mountain;
- King Bob-omb fight;
- Bob-omb explosions and Yoshi mouth;
- general occasional slowdown.

## 3DS reports

- enemies too fast;
- Bob-omb Battlefield half-speed;
- Tiny-Huge Island impossible;
- lag even with ARM9/TWL overclock for some users.

## Interpretation

The list mixes:

- insufficient CPU budget;
- fixed-step logic bugs;
- scene-specific timing;
- animation/effect bugs.

Each failure must be classified, not dismissed as generic lag.
