```text
https://www.sm64games.com/game/60-fps-patch
```

with the SM64DS community patch.

That page concerns the Nintendo 64 Super Mario 64 60 FPS hack. It is not
evidence for the Nintendo DS patch.

Correct public provenance:

```text
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJXEAIOFcNU
```

linked independently by AYN Thor and 3DS community guides and attributed to
gamemasterplc.
