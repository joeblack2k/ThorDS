# Soak and recovery

## 60-minute soak

Include:

- ordinary movement;
- stress scene;
- at least one transition;
- one pause/resume;
- one sleep/wake;
- RA Casual online when applicable.

## Monitor

- crash/ANR;
- memory;
- thermal throttling;
- semantic rate;
- ARM9 debt;
- audio underruns;
- cadence resets;
- renderer failures.

## Recovery

After soak:

- save;
- close;
- relaunch;
- load save;
- confirm mode/effective ratio;
- run five more minutes.
