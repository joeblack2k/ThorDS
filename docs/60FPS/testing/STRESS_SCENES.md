# Stress scenes

## Mandatory

- Chain Chomp proximity and attack;
- BOB mountain climb;
- King Bob-omb fight;
- explosions;
- Yoshi holding/swallowing;
- Tiny-Huge Island progression;
- water;
- flight;
- slide;
- caps;
- moving platforms;
- minigames.
