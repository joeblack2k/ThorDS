# Scope and non-goals

## In scope

- Current-path instrumentation.
- Frontend right-stick ownership.
- Smooth-camera input mapping.
- Slot-2 camera protocol.
- JNI/native transport.
- Exact EU game patch.
- R3 recenter.
- HUD/tutorial-arrow suppression.
- Camera-audio root-cause removal.
- Profile/settings integration.
- Unit, native, patch and physical tests.
- Public source and evidence.

## Out of scope

- Vertical pitch/free-look.
- 60fps gameplay.
- ARM9 overclock behavior.
- A new camera collision system.
- Level-specific camera redesign.
- Other ROM regions.
- Other games.
- Patched-ROM distribution.
- New achievement definitions.
- Broad emulator input redesign.

## Allowed compatibility work

True Widescreen, RA Casual, save/load, lifecycle and later-60fps compatibility may be tested or provided with narrow plumbing, but their core implementations must not be expanded by this camera pass.
