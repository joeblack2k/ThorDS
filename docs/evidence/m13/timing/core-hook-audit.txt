Findings:
- NDS::RunFrame() advances one complete DS display period.
- Frontend target FPS and presenter counters cannot prove unique SM64DS
  gameplay updates.
- The public 60fps patch provenance found by the audit targets NTSC-U/USA
  revision 1.1, not the target European ASMP revision 0.
- Provenance URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJXEAIOFcNU
- The public material identifies an xdelta distribution, but exposes no
  source code, AR words or reproducible binary-to-source map.
- The repository has no proven European 60fps Action Replay code or patched
  reference copy.
- No 60fps runtime code was invented or added.
- Independent decomp review confirms the same EU game-side hook and identifies
  the unresolved vtable dispatch behind `data_0209d4a8->vt[3]` and `func8`.
- The decomp also shows that animation advancement, fixed-frame actor timers,
  stage updates and message/HUD updates consume the cadence value; changing
  the VBlank threshold alone is therefore not yet a safe product patch.
- No reliable EU ASMP ROM offsets, original instruction words or conditional
  Action Replay preconditions have been established.
- The normal scene object is identified as `_ZTV5Stage`, with
  `Stage::Behavior` at `0x0202bbbc`; this narrows the semantic update
  dispatch, but does not by itself identify a safe patch word.
- Independent dispatch review distinguishes the IRQ graph callbacks from the
  semantic lifecycle: `func_02019144` uses active-object vtable slot 2 and
  `func_02019100` uses slot 3, while `Stage::Behavior` is slot 6 and
  `Stage::Render` is slot 9. `dScEntry_c::Behavior` is `0x0211a2b8` and
  `dScEntry_c::Render` is `0x0211a26c`.
- Detailed mapping is recorded in
  `docs/research/sm64ds-60fps-decomp-map.md`.
- Original EU overlay-75 function digests and offsets are recorded in
  `docs/evidence/m13/timing/eu-overlay-original-hashes.txt`; no ROM bytes are
  included.
- A read-only native/Kotlin telemetry path now samples the mapped
  `data_020a0db0` counter in one-second windows; its scope and limitations are
  recorded in
  `docs/evidence/m13/timing/game-loop-counter-instrumentation.txt`.
- The pinned European decomp now provides a concrete game-side scheduling map
  in `docs/research/sm64ds-60fps-decomp-map.md`: `IRQ::VBlankHandler` at
  `0x0201a534`, the `data_0208ee44` tick, and the render/lag callback split.
- The map also shows that scene/overlay initialization can restore the tick,
  so a one-time boot write is not an adequate implementation.
