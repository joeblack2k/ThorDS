M7 W-03 Castle Garden side-region culling probe
Date: 2026-08-01
Scope: developer widescreen probe, fixed Castle Garden movement

Result: PARTIAL

Route:
- Castle Garden/castle grounds is the sole M7 representative scene.
- The existing app-private checkpoint loaded through the direct-path debug
  route with `success=1`.
- Slot-2 analog input was held at `x=1,y=0` for exactly 120 emulator frames,
  then neutralized at `x=0,y=0`.
- A Vulkan dense live burst requested only `screen` captured `120/120`
  frames with frame IDs `845..964`, one-frame steps and no capture failures.

Burst integrity:
- Every frame decoded as an opaque `256x384` RGBA PNG:
  `98,304/98,304` pixels had alpha `255`.
- Visible non-black pixels ranged from `97,786` to `97,789` per frame; no
  blank or fully black frame occurred.
- `59/119` adjacent pairs were byte-for-byte equal and `60/119` changed.
  The repeated pairs follow the renderer's observed duplicate cadence; they
  are not treated as a culling failure.
- The 120-frame run visibly advanced the player, animation and camera. A
  final primary screenshot showed upright terrain, side geometry and dust
  without a visible black hole or broken edge.

Final-primary follow-up:
- A separate final-primary sample captured `16` screenshots at `1920x1080`
  while the same Castle Garden movement input was held. The host-side polling
  interval was approximately `80 ms`; these images were not tied to explicit
  one-frame emulator-step acknowledgements.
- The native 4:3 safe rectangle was mapped to `x=240..1679`. The left outer
  ROI `x=0..239,y=45..1035` and the right outer ROI
  `x=1680..1859,y=45..1035` were non-black in all 16 samples and changed
  across all 15 adjacent sample pairs. The rightmost Android edge handle was
  excluded from the measured right ROI.
- Visual inspection showed upright Castle Garden terrain in both side regions
  and player movement between the first and last sample. This is useful
  final-primary side-presence evidence, but the sample does not identify and
  track one game-world landmark at exact emulator-frame boundaries.

Exact-step final-primary follow-up:
- After dismissing a Thor anti-image-retention overlay, a warmed sequence
  captured `30/30` final-primary screenshots using one explicit
  `STEP_FRAMES 1` acknowledgement per image. The earlier overlay-containing
  attempt was discarded and is not evidence.
- Every retained image was a valid `1920x1080` Castle Garden frame with
  `2,073,600` non-black pixels. No adjacent pair was byte-identical.
- Subsampled measurements of the left and right outer ROIs changed in all
  `29/29` adjacent pairs. The camera pan visibly moved the castle wall,
  trees, hill and path through the side regions. No single landmark remained
  wholly inside one side ROI for the full sequence, so this does not yet
  satisfy the semantic-landmark tracking requirement.

Measurement boundary:
- The `256x384` burst is the native DS composition: its top half is already
  the complete native `256x192` 4:3 image. It therefore has no pixels outside
  the final primary surface's 4:3 safe rectangle.
- Fixed raw edge-strip diagnostics were still non-empty and changing on the
  left (`gradient min=48, max=480, median=109`; 60/119 changing pairs).
  These are internal DS edge strips, not a final-primary side-region proof.
- The right edge strip remained filled but had median gradient `0`; no
  semantic animated landmark was established there.

Decision:
- This advances W-03 from no movement evidence to `PARTIAL`: the fixed
  Castle Garden movement and 120-frame capture are reproducible, intact and
  visually coherent, and the final-primary sample confirms changing side
  content outside the mapped native rectangle. The clean 30-frame exact-step
  primary sequence strengthens that result.
- W-03 is not a pass. The remaining proof must capture an identifiable
  animated landmark in the final 16:9 primary side region across consecutive
  exact-step frames, with the native 4:3 bounds overlaid for comparison.
- W-01 formal round/square repeat, W-04 HUD, W-05 glyph, W-06 bottom geometry
  and W-20 world-to-pause-to-world transition proof remain open.
